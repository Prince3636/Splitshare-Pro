💸 SplitShare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026
Your Privacy Commitment: SplitShare is built to make splitting group expenses transparent and fair. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we access your bank accounts or store UPI PINs. All QR code generation is performed 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the SplitShare application, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Credentials: Name, username, email address, password hash, and optional profile avatar.
  • Contact Information: Optional mobile phone number for account lookup and peer identification.
  • Payment Details: Virtual Payment Address (UPI ID) and optional custom QR code image URLs that you voluntarily provide to receive peer-to-peer repayments.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Group names, bill expense descriptions, amounts, split ratios, approval statuses, and debt settlements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate mathematical bill splits, group balances, and minimum-cash-flow debt simplification.
  • To generate direct NPCI UPI payment deep links (`upi://pay`) allowing you to open your preferred banking app (Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe) with prepopulated amounts.
  • To send group invitation emails and bill approval notifications.
  • To maintain account security, prevent fraudulent activity, and enforce session authentication.

3. Payment Security & UPI Handling

SplitShare is not a payment gateway or bank. We never process monetary transfers directly, nor do we request, handle, or store your UPI PIN, debit card numbers, CVVs, or OTPs. All actual money transfers occur directly between users inside third-party authorized UPI banking applications.

4. Data Storage & Multi-Tenant Protection

Your data is stored securely in encrypted cloud infrastructure. Multi-tenancy access controls ensure that only authenticated members of a specific group can view expenses, settlement summaries, and receipt records associated with that group.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time via My UPI / Profile → Delete Account & Purge Data.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the mobile application, you can submit an instant deletion request via our public web portal at Account Deletion Page.

Upon confirmation, your profile, authentication credentials, and active group memberships are permanently removed from our production databases.
